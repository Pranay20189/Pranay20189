### 01. Hyperlocal Service Marketplace

<sub>Full Stack Web Application</sub>

A web-based platform developed to connect customers with local service providers. Customers can discover services, book appointments and manage their bookings, while workers can manage service requests.

**Key Features**

- **Role-Based Authentication:** Separate registration and login functionality for customers and service providers.
- **Service Discovery:** Customers can browse and find available local services.
- **Booking Management:** Book services with preferred date, time, duration and address.
- **Appointment Management:** Workers can accept or reject booking requests and update booking status.
- **Worker Profiles:** Service providers can manage their skills, pricing and availability.
- **Ratings & Reviews:** Customers can share feedback about completed services.
- **Admin Management:** Supports administrative functions for managing the platform.
